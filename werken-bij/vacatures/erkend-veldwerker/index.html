Certified fieldworker

32/36/40 hours – Throughout the Netherlands

As a BRL 2003 certified fieldworker, you have your boots in the clay. You carry out measurements in all weathers and make sure theory becomes practice. Reliable water bed investigation starts with your accurate measurements, both qualitative and quantitative. But measuring alone is not enough. It takes craftsmanship, sharpness and the ability to make the right judgement on site in accordance with the applicable guidelines.

At the same time, you take the lead. You safeguard quality, guide colleagues in the field and train new fieldworkers towards their certification. In this way, you actively help build a strong, professional fieldwork team. What you deliver forms the basis for our advice and, ultimately, for sustainable regional water management in the Netherlands. That responsibility suits you. Do you want to do more than just carry out tasks? Do you want to make an impact on the quality of our work and on the development of your colleagues? Then we are looking for you!

What will you do?

As a certified fieldworker, you take the lead in the field. You work on large and varied water bed investigations throughout the Netherlands. You make sure the work is carried out according to protocol and that quality is safeguarded. You:

  • independently carry out water bed investigations in accordance with BRL 2003 and applicable protocols.
  • supervise fellow fieldworkers on site.
  • safeguard quality, safety and compliance with guidelines.
  • sample water beds and record field data in our own app, W.I.T.
  • carry out measurements in watercourses and determine sediment layering and dredging volumes.
  • work with a GPS, measuring equipment and a tablet.
  • liaise with advisers and project managers about progress and particulars.

You are the eyes and ears of the adviser. Without your work, there is no reliable advice. You raise the fieldwork team to a higher level. We have many talented fieldworkers. What we need now is someone who provides direction.

As an experienced BRL 2003 certified fieldworker, you:

  • guide and coach your colleagues in the field.
  • train new fieldworkers towards their certification.
  • share your knowledge of sampling methods, interpretation and quality assurance.
  • address colleagues on safety and carefulness.
  • contribute ideas on how we can organise our fieldwork more smartly and efficiently.

You are not just an executor, but also a role model. You help us build a professional, strong and proud fieldwork team. Moving forward sustainably, together.

Who are you?

You are experienced, precise and know how important your role is within the project as a whole. You work independently, but feel responsible for the team and the end result.

You have:

  • a completed secondary vocational education (MBO) qualification (water management, infrastructure, nature & green space or similar).
  • a valid BRL 2003 certification.
  • demonstrable experience with water bed investigation.
  • knowledge of sampling methods and field recording.
  • leadership in the field and the ambition to train colleagues.
  • good communication skills.
  • a category B driving licence.

What do we offer?

At Tijhuis Ingenieurs, we take fieldwork seriously. You get space, trust and responsibility.

  • A position for 32, 36 or 40 hours. The choice is yours!
  • A salary in line with your experience, plus holiday allowance and year-end bonus.
  • Travel time = working time (these hours are paid).
  • A work van, phone and tablet.
  • 26 days of annual leave (based on 40 hours).
  • Pension scheme with the Stichting Bedrijfstakpensioenfonds Waterbouw.
  • A personal training plan.
  • Room to develop yourself further as both a specialist and a mentor.
  • Budget for fun company outings through our activities club.

Who are we?

At Tijhuis Ingenieurs, we believe in a more beautiful Netherlands for present and future generations. As pioneers in regional water, we strive for sustainable solutions for a resilient future. Our passion drives us to provide sustainable advice in and around the water, with our core business of 'water beds and dredging' as our cornerstone. With our close-knit team of experts, we never shy away from a project. We are determined to create a lasting, positive impact with our expertise, because we believe in the power of a water-resilient Netherlands.

From our offices in Hoorn, Amersfoort and Joure, 64 colleagues work on projects across the country. The team consists of fieldworkers, water (bed) consultants, GIS specialists, supervisors and project managers. Our organisation has an open, familial atmosphere with plenty of room for personal initiative and creativity.

Frequently asked questions about working as a Certified Field Technician

What does a working day as a certified field technician look like?

No two days are the same. You carry out water bed investigations at various project locations, coordinate special circumstances with advisers and guide colleagues in the field. You are the eyes and ears of the adviser and ensure that the data collected forms a reliable basis for the rest of the project.

What is the difference between a field technician and a certified field technician?

As a certified field technician, you have additional responsibility for the quality of the fieldwork. You monitor compliance with the applicable protocols, share your expertise and help less experienced colleagues to develop further.

What role do I play in the development of the fieldwork team?

You provide direction to a team of talented field technicians. You guide colleagues, share your practical experience and help the team to further improve the quality of the fieldwork. In this way, you lift not only the work but also your colleagues to a higher level.

Which techniques and tools will I work with?

Among other things, you use GPS, measuring equipment and a tablet to investigate water beds. You record measurement data and special observations in W.I.T., our own application. This makes the results available for the further development of projects.
