Junior water bed advisor

32/40 hours – Hoorn/Amersfoort

Are you curious about the world of dredging and water beds and looking for an exciting challenge? Then read on!

What will you do?

As a water bed advisor, you are the first point of contact for our clients. You will work on a variety of issues relating to water beds and dredging. You advise our clients on the investigations to be carried out and the follow-up steps to be taken. You are involved in the entire investigation process, from the quotation through to the delivery of the final report. Your work consists primarily of carrying out water bed investigations and drawing up dredging plans. Within a project, you will soon be working independently. This includes:

  • Carrying out (preliminary) investigations and drawing up investigation and analysis plans;
  • Preparing and supervising fieldwork;
  • Interpreting and processing field and laboratory results;
  • Drafting and discussing reports;
  • Providing follow-up advice;
  • Preparing quotations;
  • Managing fieldworkers and GIS staff.

Who are you?

You are a motivated and proactive colleague. You are practical and able to switch gears quickly. You are inquisitive, eager to learn and up to date with developments in our field. You work independently, but are also a good team player. You have:

  • a completed relevant university of applied sciences (HBO) or university (WO) degree, for example in water management or environmental science;
  • relevant work experience is an advantage;
  • good communication skills.

What do we offer?

  • A dynamic position with a lot of contact with people.
  • A good salary, depending on your experience, plus holiday allowance and year-end bonus.
  • A pension scheme with the Stichting Bedrijfstakpensioenfonds Waterbouw.
  • The option to purchase certificates so you can share in the profit generated.
  • A holiday entitlement scheme for employees aged 50 and over.
  • A phone and laptop so you can carry out your work properly.
  • 26 holiday days based on 40 hours.
  • A budget for enjoyable company activities through our activities club.

Who are we?

We are Tijhuis Ingenieurs, an engineering consultancy specialising in improving quality in and around water. We work on well-functioning water systems, clean surface water and attractive, nature-friendly banks.

Our roots lie in water beds and dredging. Much can be achieved with dredging and redevelopment, but it is not always the only solution. That is why we look beyond dredging alone. We investigate how water, water bed, banks, ecology, design and management influence one another and which combination of measures is actually needed.

From our offices in Hoorn and Amersfoort, around 75 colleagues work on projects throughout the Netherlands. Our organisation has an open, family atmosphere with short lines of communication and plenty of room for personal initiative.
