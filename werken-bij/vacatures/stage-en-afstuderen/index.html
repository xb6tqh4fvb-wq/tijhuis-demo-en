Internships and graduation projects

Internships and graduation projects at Tijhuis Ingenieurs

Are you ready to turn your knowledge into impactful practical experience? At Tijhuis Ingenieurs, a family business with more than 30 years of expertise in sustainable regional water management, we offer you the chance to develop your talents further. Become part of a close-knit team of more than 60 professionals and help build a cleaner and more resilient Netherlands together.

Why choose Tijhuis Ingenieurs?

  • Pioneers in sustainability: watch our new video about 'Sustainability',
  • Learn from the best: work with experienced experts who are happy to help you grow and take your ideas to the next level,
  • Friendly, family atmosphere: with us you are not just an intern, but a valued team member. We work with short lines of communication, are always engaged, and you can enjoy fun company outings,
  • Accredited training company: we offer high-quality supervision and an environment where you, as a student, truly make a contribution.

What can you expect?

  • Diverse range: We work across four different fields, which allows us to take an integrated approach to achieving our mission of a better living environment for people and animals. Find your interest within this diverse range of activities and challenges,
  • Personal guidance: receive guidance from experts in your field who put your development first. In our experience, interns are very satisfied with the guidance they receive at Tijhuis Ingenieurs,
  • Real impact: your work contributes directly to the living environment of future generations in the Netherlands. Feel free to see this for yourself by heading out with our team in the field.
