Project Manager Water Bed

32/40 hours – Hoorn/Amersfoort/Joure
Mid-level/Senior

Do you have experience with water bed projects and do you want to work on a sustainable living environment? Then read on!

What will you do?

As a project manager, you are the first point of contact for our clients. You advise on (policy-related) issues in the field of water, water beds and landscape design. You analyse how the water system functions in terms of various aspects, such as management, maintenance and potential. Of course, you also work on concrete plans and designs. Above all, we consider it important that you want to contribute to a more sustainable living environment. Your duties include:

  • Managing the project team and working together with field technicians and GIS staff;
  • Correctly interpreting the investigation strategies in the plans drawn up;
  • Analysing water systems in an integrated way, with an eye for sustainability and spatial quality;
  • Preparing water bed investigations, policy and management plans and technical reports;
  • Contributing to tenders and writing quotations;
  • Supervising work during implementation, such as dredging and bank works;
  • Providing follow-up advice and identifying future opportunities.

Who are you?

We are looking for an experienced adviser/project manager who is well versed in our field. You are up to date with the latest developments and can look at a water system in an integrated way. You establish the relationship between management, maintenance and functioning of the water system. In our work, it helps if you are practical and can switch quickly between tasks. You are also a motivated and proactive colleague. Your knowledge and experience make an important contribution to our team of advisers. You are an adviser with:

  • at least 3 years of experience as a project manager in the water (bed) sector, preferably at a commercial consultancy;
  • a relevant, completed degree (university of applied sciences or university);
  • good communication skills;
  • a preference for someone who has to travel no more than 1 hour to our office in Hoorn, Amersfoort or Joure, for the sake of your own (mental) health and the environment.

What do we offer?

At Tijhuis Ingenieurs, you will work in an organisation where everyone is equally important, from field technicians to management. We have a great, varied team of people who are happy to give you a hand. No ad hoc meetings, just a quick walk over to your colleague to ask how things stand. We also love active outings, walks during breaks and the occasional day working from home. Of course, we offer you a package with attractive terms of employment:

  • A position of 32 to 40 hours in a motivated and friendly team.
  • Excellent training and career development opportunities.
  • A good salary, depending on your experience, plus holiday allowance and end-of-year bonus.
  • Pension scheme with the Stichting Bedrijfstakpensioenfonds Waterbouw.
  • 26 holiday days based on 40 hours.
  • A laptop and other equipment needed to carry out your work properly.
  • A good work-life balance thanks to our 'flexible working policy' and flexible arrangements for working hours and schedules.

Who are we?

At Tijhuis Ingenieurs, we believe in a Netherlands that is ready for the challenges of climate change through effective water management, so that we create a lasting positive impact. Our passion drives us as the expert in sustainable regional water management, with our core business of 'water beds and dredging' as a pillar. Think of a well-functioning water system, clean surface water and attractive, nature-friendly banks. We prepare dredging projects and supervise the work, but we also carry out complete redevelopments of areas. Our projects contribute to a safe and attractive living environment. We work mainly for municipalities and water boards.

From our offices in Hoorn, Amersfoort and Joure, 64 colleagues work on projects across the country. The team consists of fieldworkers, water (bed) consultants, GIS specialists, supervisors and project managers. Our organisation has an open, familial atmosphere with plenty of room for personal initiative and creativity.

Frequently asked questions about working as a Water Bed Project Manager

What does a working day as a water bed project manager look like?

No two days are the same. You consult with clients, discuss progress with your project team and work on advice or plans for water bed projects. In doing so, you switch between technical issues, project coordination and practical implementation.

Will I mainly be doing project management, or will I also be involved in the technical side?

Both! You are responsible for managing projects, but you also remain involved in the technical content. You assess survey results, interpret survey strategies and translate findings into concrete advice and measures.

Which phases of a project will I be involved in?

You are involved in various phases, from investigation and planning to tendering and implementation. This means you see how your advice is translated into concrete work in and around the water.

What makes the role of water bed project manager at Tijhuis Ingenieurs special?

You look beyond the water bed alone. Together with colleagues from different disciplines, you work on integrated solutions. This means you work on projects in which water bed investigation, sustainable water management and the design of the living environment come together.
