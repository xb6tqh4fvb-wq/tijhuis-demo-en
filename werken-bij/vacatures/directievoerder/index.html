Contract Supervisor

32/40 hours - Hoorn/Amersfoort

Are you the link between client and contractor? You work independently or as part of a team on projects that have an impact on the living environment. Sound like you? Then read on!

What will you do?

As a contract supervisor, you are the link between client and contractor. You oversee the technical realisation, the work process and the communication between all parties. Depending on the project, you work independently or within a project team on assignments in the civil engineering and/or environmental sector. You carry out the work assigned to you and make sure the project runs smoothly and professionally. You do this from our offices, but of course also at project locations throughout the Netherlands. Your daily tasks are:

  • Internal and external responsibility for delivering the project;
  • Monitoring the planning of the work of the contract supervisor and site inspector (workload);
  • Carrying out initial, interim and final inspections and checking that the project is being carried out in accordance with the;
  • Contract/specification;
  • Maintaining contact with the client;
  • Maintaining contact with the contractor.

Who are you?

You have good communication skills and are good at planning and organising. It is important that you understand our sector and are up to date with current developments. We also like to call on your commercial ability: you can draw up and justify a good quote! You are a contract supervisor with:

  • a completed relevant higher professional education (HBO) degree or so much experience that you have an equivalent level of thinking and working;
  • 2 – 5 years of relevant work experience, preferably as a contract supervisor;
  • a healthy dose of independence;
  • good communication skills;
  • knowledge of the civil engineering sector and the associated systems and publications;
  • a category B driving licence and VCA-VOL certification.

What do we offer?

  • A dynamic position with a lot of contact with people.
  • A good salary, depending on your experience, plus holiday allowance and year-end bonus.
  • A pension scheme with the Stichting Bedrijfstakpensioenfonds Waterbouw.
  • The option to purchase certificates so you can share in the profit generated.
  • A holiday entitlement scheme for employees aged 50 and over.
  • 26 holiday days based on 40 hours.
  • A car, phone and laptop so you can do your work properly.
  • Travel expenses reimbursement from 10 to 30 kilometres one way.
  • A budget for enjoyable company activities through our activities club.

Who are we?

At Tijhuis Ingenieurs, we believe in a more beautiful Netherlands for present and future generations. As pioneers in regional water, we strive for sustainable solutions for a resilient future. Our passion drives us to provide sustainable advice in and around the water, with our core business of 'water beds and dredging' as our cornerstone. With our close-knit team of experts, we never shy away from a project. We are determined to create a lasting, positive impact with our expertise, because we believe in the power of a water-resilient Netherlands.

From our offices in Hoorn, Amersfoort and Joure, 60 colleagues work on projects across the country. The team consists of fieldworkers, water (bed) advisers, GIS staff, site inspectors and contract supervisors. Our organisation has an open, family-like atmosphere with plenty of room for personal initiative and creativity.

Frequently asked questions about working as a contract supervisor

What does a contract supervisor do at Tijhuis Ingenieurs?
As a contract supervisor, you oversee the implementation of projects in the civil engineering and environmental sector. You monitor the planning, check that work is carried out in accordance with the contract and specification, and liaise with clients, contractors, site inspectors and colleagues.

What kinds of projects will I work on as a contract supervisor?
You work on projects in the field of water beds, dredging, banks, civil engineering structures and regional water management. The projects often have a direct impact on the living environment and are carried out for, for example, municipalities, water boards and other clients.

Will I mainly work outdoors or in the office?
You combine working in the office with project visits throughout the Netherlands. Outdoors, you monitor and inspect the work being carried out. In the office, you work on documentation, liaise with the client and discuss bottlenecks or practical solutions with contractors and colleagues.

Do I need experience with water beds or dredging work?
Experience with water beds or dredging work is valuable, but not always essential. More important is that you have experience in construction supervision, implementation projects, contracts, specifications and collaboration within the civil engineering (GWW) and/or environmental sector.

Is VCA-VOL mandatory for this position?
VCA-VOL is desirable for this position. If you do not yet have the certificate, we will make sure you obtain it.

Can I work from Hoorn, Amersfoort or Joure?
Yes, you can work from our offices in Hoorn, Amersfoort or Joure. In addition, you will regularly be present at project sites across the country.

Will I get a car, phone and laptop?
Yes, for this position you will receive a car, phone and laptop so you can carry out your work properly.
