Works Planner

32/40 hours – Hoorn/Amersfoort

Do you have a passion for dredging projects and bank works, and do you want to contribute to a sustainable living environment? We are looking for an experienced works planner who takes on all phases of a project and guides it to a successful result.

What will you do?

As a works planner, you are the first point of contact for our clients. You will work on a variety of issues in the fields of dredging, civil engineering structures and bank constructions. You advise our clients on the dredging projects that are going ahead, and you are involved in the entire process: from writing the specification to tendering and the final work! Your tasks include:

  • Preparing dredging and bank works;
  • Designing technical structures for bank developments, jetties and quays;
  • Interpreting and processing fieldwork data and structural calculations;
  • Writing specifications for dredging and hydraulic engineering works;
  • Drawing up reports and cost estimates;
  • Working with consultants, fieldworkers and supervisors;
  • Supervising works in progress (optional).

Who are you?

You are an experienced professional in civil engineering, water management or environmental science. Tijhuis Ingenieurs offers a challenging role as a works planner, in which strong technical skills and the ability to coordinate projects fully are essential. With your communication skills and proactive attitude, you translate recent developments into projects that appeal to both us and our clients. You have:

  • a completed and relevant higher professional education (HBO) or university degree, preferably in civil engineering, water management or environmental science with civil engineering knowledge;
  • you are a skilled communicator;
  • you have at least 2 years of relevant work experience;
  • knowledge of AutoCAD is an advantage;
  • you work well in a team and take initiative.

What do we offer?

  • A dynamic role with plenty of contact with people.
  • A good salary, depending on your experience, plus holiday allowance and an end-of-year bonus.
  • A pension scheme with the Stichting Bedrijfstakpensioenfonds Waterbouw.
  • The opportunity to buy certificates and share in the profits made.
  • A holiday scheme for employees aged 50 and over.
  • A phone and laptop so you can do your job properly.
  • 26 days of annual leave based on a 40-hour week.
  • A budget for enjoyable company activities through our activities club.

Who are we?

At Tijhuis Ingenieurs, we believe in a more beautiful Netherlands for present and future generations. As pioneers in regional water, we strive for sustainable solutions for a resilient future. Our passion drives us to provide sustainable advice in and around the water, with our core business of 'water beds and dredging' as our cornerstone. With our close-knit team of experts, we never shy away from a project. We are determined to create a lasting, positive impact with our expertise, because we believe in the power of a water-resilient Netherlands.

From our offices in Hoorn, Amersfoort and Joure, 64 colleagues work on projects across the country. The team consists of fieldworkers, water (bed) consultants, GIS specialists, supervisors and project managers. Our organisation has an open, familial atmosphere with plenty of room for personal initiative and creativity.

Frequently asked questions about working as a Works Planner

What does a working day as a works planner look like?

No two days are the same. You work on technical designs, draw up specifications or discuss progress with a client. In between, you liaise with colleagues about survey data, schedules and project preparation. This way, you combine technical work with plenty of contact with people.

As a works planner, am I only involved in the preparation?

No, you are involved in various phases of a project, from technical preparation and drawing up specifications through to tendering. Depending on the project, you may also be involved in supervising the execution of the works.

What kind of technical designs will I work on?

Among other things, you work on designs for bank structures, jetties and quays. In doing so, you translate technical data and structural calculations into solutions that suit the situation and the client's wishes.

Which colleagues will I work with?

You will work closely with advisers, fieldworkers and supervisors. The data collected outdoors forms an important basis for your work. In this way, you link the research phase with the technical preparation of a project.
