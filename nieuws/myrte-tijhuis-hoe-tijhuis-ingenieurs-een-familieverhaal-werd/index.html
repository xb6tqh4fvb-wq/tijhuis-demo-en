Myrte Tijhuis – How Tijhuis Ingenieurs became a family story

Moving forward sustainably together since 1996

In 2026 we are celebrating a special anniversary: Tijhuis Ingenieurs is 30 years old. A milestone that I look back on with pride, and look ahead from! Because this company was not built by one entrepreneur, but by all the colleagues who have contributed ideas, worked hard and grown along with it over the past 30 years . That is precisely why we had a festive logo designed for this anniversary year!

Built on trust and connection

The foundation of Tijhuis Ingenieurs lies in our family. Together we built this company. Not from a distance, but out of commitment, trust and the idea that work and private life can reinforce each other. My father always had a clear entrepreneurial drive. After starting his career at Oranjewoud, he decided to follow his own path. As a family, we were part of it from the very first moment. My mother played a crucial, but often invisible, role in this. She kept everything running at home, so that my father's entrepreneurship never felt like an 'extra'. It simply belonged.

The blue Renault: a childhood full of memories

Sometimes we children were allowed to come along into the field. We would take water bed samples in our wellies, then head home in my parents' blue Renault. In my office in Hoorn there is still a painting we made as children, with that blue Renault proudly depicted on it. We gave it to my father at the opening of his premises on the Corantijn in 1999. Looking back, we were capturing a piece of history without realising it. Even as a child, that opening felt like a milestone and it still does.

Growing up in a company that feels like home

As children we also often helped out at the office: folding maps, stuffing envelopes with board money or helping to put together Christmas hampers. On school study days and public holidays we would sit at a desk or drink hot chocolate from the vending machine. They are small memories, but they say a lot about the atmosphere: practical, warm and always together. That feeling has stayed with us to this day.

Roles that grow over the years

As we grew older, our roles grew with us. My sister Iris joined the company in her twenties. She progressed from GIS officer to operations coordinator. That is typical of Tijhuis Ingenieurs: it is not only about engineering here, but above all about people who grow in their work. My own path followed a little later. I have worked at Tijhuis Ingenieurs since 2017 and over the years I have grown into the role of owner, a role I now share with Lennart.

Moving forward with vision

Looking back now, I see not only how my father has always remained an entrepreneur, but also how the field has changed. Where it used to be mainly about management and maintenance, it is now increasingly about restoration, innovation and future-proofing. Although I have found my father very innovative for 30 years now!

Sustainable regional water management is at the heart of my vision for the future of Tijhuis Ingenieurs. We look beyond engineering alone. In every project we map out our clients' sustainability ambitions and translate them into practical, integrated solutions. Always with an eye for the client's needs and what nature requires.

Looking back to move forward

For me, 2026 is above all a year of stories. Stories about how it all began. About the blue Renault, the painting and hot chocolate, but also about growth, change and the future. About the colleagues who make this company what it is. That is why we will be sharing more stories this year, from past and present. Because only by looking back can we move forward sustainably together.

Contact
