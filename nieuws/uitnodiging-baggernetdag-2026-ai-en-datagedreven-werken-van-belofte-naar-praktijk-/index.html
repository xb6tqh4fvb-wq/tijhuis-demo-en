Invitation to Baggernetdag 2026: ‘AI and data-driven working: from promise to practice’

On Thursday 19 November, Platform Baggernet and Tijhuis Ingenieurs are organising Baggernetdag 2026, focusing on AI and data-driven working. A day of practical examples, knowledge exchange and a visit to a unique project in Veenendaal. We invite you to join us.

What opportunities does data-driven working offer?

In the morning programme, various organisations share their experiences with innovative projects and pilots. Not future scenarios, but concrete examples of what AI and data can already mean today. We present new possibilities, smarter planning, more efficient working and better-founded choices in water management. What do these developments deliver in practice? Which applications work and where do the challenges lie?

A unique bank strategy in Veenendaal

In the afternoon programme, we present the bank strategy that Tijhuis Ingenieurs developed for Waterschap Vallei en Veluwe. Stakeholder management and nature development were very important here. Stakeholder management is central because of the many households involved, and considerable attention was paid to nature development in the redesign of the banks. We discuss the practical, joint implementation and the many decision points in the project.
