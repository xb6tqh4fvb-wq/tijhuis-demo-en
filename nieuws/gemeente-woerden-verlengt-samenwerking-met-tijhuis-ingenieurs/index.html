4 December 2025

Municipality of Woerden extends its cooperation with Tijhuis Ingenieurs

Tijhuis Ingenieurs will once again be responsible for the management and maintenance of the watercourses of the municipalities of Woerden and Oudewater in the coming years. With this new framework agreement, we are building on almost 15 years of cooperation in which trust, quality and an integrated approach are central, and we are proud of that.

Complete care for the water system

Under the agreement, we are responsible for, among other things:

  • drawing up and updating the (multi-year) dredging schedule;
  • liaising with other parties, such as the water board and contractors;
  • maintaining the water system in good condition;
  • taking up and handling reports from residents and emergencies;
  • an integrated (dredging) approach in which we look, among other things, at the options for creating nature-friendly banks.

Tijs van Wegberg, project manager at Tijhuis Ingenieurs, says:

We think along with you, take work off your hands and always keep one goal in sight: a future-proof water system. 

From dredging to flowering banks

Over the past few years, together with the municipality of Woerden and Stichting Bloeiende Oevers, we have already created more than 500 metres of nature-friendly bank. The new agreement enables us to continue this work. This means we can keep working over the coming years towards greater biodiversity and a living environment that residents can be proud of.
