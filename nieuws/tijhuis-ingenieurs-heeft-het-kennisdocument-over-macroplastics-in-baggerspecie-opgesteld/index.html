18 July 2025

Tijhuis Ingenieurs has drawn up the knowledge document on macroplastics in dredged sediment

Large quantities of macroplastics, such as packaging, bags and other litter, accumulate in and around watercourses. This plastic ends up in the dredged sediment, where it harms ecosystems. It also makes the execution of dredging works and the processing of dredged sediment considerably more difficult.

To map and tackle the problem, Tijhuis Ingenieurs has written a knowledge document commissioned by the Dutch Association of Water Boards (Unie van Waterschappen), Rijkswaterstaat and SIKB. The knowledge document is a practical working method that offers guidance for recognising, recording and responsibly handling macroplastics in dredged sediment. This document has been published by SIKB and is available to everyone in the field.

The working method in the knowledge document can be put into practice immediately by consultancies, contractors, water managers and regulators. It is already being applied in practice.

 

Knowledge document on macroplastics in dredged sediment

The knowledge document was drawn up by Tijhuis Ingenieurs, commissioned by the Dutch Association of Water Boards (Unie van Waterschappen), Rijkswaterstaat and SIKB. A committee with representatives from these organisations contributed ideas on the content of the document.
