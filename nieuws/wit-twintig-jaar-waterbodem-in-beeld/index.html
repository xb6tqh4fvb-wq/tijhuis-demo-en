WIT: Twenty years of bringing the water bed into view

WIT was developed in 2007 as software for planning dredging work. Since then, the system has grown into a digital platform that supports the entire dredging process: from multi-year planning and measurements to project preparation, implementation and completion.

For municipalities and water boards, WIT brings together information on watercourses, sludge thicknesses, schedules, quantities and costs in a clear overview. Indoors this is done on a computer, outdoors on a tablet. This allows users to view project information and draw up schedules in the office, while measurement data, checks and specific observations from the field are recorded immediately.

One database for every project phase

The modules within WIT work together from one central database. As a result, data does not have to be entered again each time, but remains available for the next step in the process.

Measurement data forms the basis for determining the need for dredging, setting priorities and calculating quantities and costs. During implementation, this information is supplemented with up-to-date progress and points of attention from the field.

This keeps project information up to date and ensures that everyone involved works with the same data. It helps to maintain an overview, to substantiate decisions and to easily find information again later.

From planning to implementation

WIT supports users in various tasks within the dredging process. Watercourses and project information can be viewed clearly on the map. Clear legends, customisable tables and different screen views mean that users can work with the information that is relevant to their role.

Multiple projects can also be viewed side by side, and different schedules and costs can be calculated over several years. From this broad overview, work can then continue towards preparation and implementation.

Completely renewed

To prepare WIT for future developments, the system has been completely renewed. Not as a refreshed version of the existing software, but rebuilt from the foundations up.

Over the years, more and more functions have been added. That made WIT more complete, but also more complex to develop further. With a modern technical basis, more computing power and a more logical structure, the system is now laid out more clearly and is better prepared for new functionalities.

Developed with user experience

The renewal was not approached from a purely technical angle. Programmers handle the build, while advisers, data specialists and account management contribute and test from their day-to-day practice.

Users' experiences and feedback have also been incorporated into the design. As a result, the new WIT is better aligned with the work involved in dredging management and with the information needs of different users.

Ready for the next phase

After twenty years of bringing the water bed into view, WIT is ready for its next step. The system supports the dredging process from planning to implementation and helps municipalities and water boards to keep project information central, up to date and usable.

With the new WIT, we are building on many years of practical experience: more modern in technology, more logical in use and designed to grow with new requirements, data sources and applications within water bed management.
