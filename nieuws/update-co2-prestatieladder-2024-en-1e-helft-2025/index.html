CO2 Performance Ladder update 2024 and first half of 2025

Sustainability in the chain

As we are certified at level 5, we continue to focus on how we can encourage sustainability in our chain. This means we try to help the companies we work with to make sustainable choices.

For example, in the first half of 2025 we did the following:

  • Supporting fellow companies and other interested parties with sustainable choices within our field and with certification on the CO2 Performance Ladder.
  • Answering questions from water boards about sustainability requirements in specifications.
  • Developing a calculation tool to calculate the emissions of equipment. • A trial with an electric fieldwork van, which proved successful! Using electric fieldwork vans contributes to our CO2 Performance Ladder objectives.
  • Myrte has become chair of the Circulair Bagger Consortium.

We do not focus only on our own emissions, but on how we can work more sustainably together with our clients. In addition to the solutions above, we always take ecology and water quality into account in dredging projects.

Emissions and targets 2024

Within scopes 1 and 2, we achieved our target relative to the growth of Tijhuis Ingenieurs (relative emissions). Absolute emissions have also fallen compared with 2023. In the charts below you can see both the total emissions per year and the emissions by category for 2024.

 

In 2024 we had emissions of 2,287 tonnes of CO2 in scope 3. However, we managed to reduce 267 tonnes of CO2 by spreading more dredged sediment. Our aim was to reduce at least 40% of our scope 1 & 2 emissions this way, which meant we had to achieve a 60-tonne CO2 reduction in scope 3. We comfortably achieved that!

Emissions in the first half of 2025

In the first half of 2025 we emitted 68.4 tonnes of CO2 within scope 1 & 2. The forecast is therefore that we will reduce our CO2 emissions in 2025. This puts us on track towards our reduction target of 28% based on our relative emissions. This is shown in the chart below.

Projects with tendering advantage

In 2024 we carried out 10 projects with a tendering advantage based on the CO2 Performance Ladder. The combined emissions of these projects were 14.7 tonnes of CO2. In 2025 we currently have 2 projects running with a tendering advantage; these projects have not yet been completed. The combined emissions of these projects are therefore not yet known.

Fun fact

In addition to reducing our total emissions, we have cut emissions by electrifying our passenger cars. The next step is electrifying the fieldwork vans. The first van was taken into use at the end of June 2025!

More information?

In our recently updated CO2 Performance Ladder manual you can find all the information on this subject.
