Water beds and dredging

We know everything about dredging and water beds. From water bed investigation to specifications and supervision of the dredging work. With more than 30 years of experience, we are a valued partner of various water boards, municipalities and provinces.

Read more

Banks and civil engineering structures

Banks and revetments are important for a good and safe water system. We survey the condition of banks and civil engineering structures, draw up management plans and design new (nature-friendly) banks, revetments and structures.

Read more

Water quality and biodiversity

Improving water quality and increasing the ecological value of the water system is a fine example of an integrated project approach. In some cases, this can even be cleverly combined with maintenance dredging. We provide practical advice and design new natural habitats.

Read more

W.I.T. and data processing

Tijhuis Ingenieurs has developed the online management system for dredging maintenance: WIT. This application is built for the collection, processing, analysis, visualisation and storage of all water bed information.

Read more

The specialist in sustainable regional water management

We are Tijhuis Ingenieurs, the experts in sustainable regional water management. We provide advice in and around the water. Our consultants think along with you about the best approach for your project. Our speciality is preparing and supervising dredging work, but our activities do not stop there. Remediation, bank development, ecology, water quality and civil engineering structures also fall within our fields of expertise. With our own online management system, we can take on routine maintenance work in a structured way. This ensures efficient management of the water system, now and in the future.
