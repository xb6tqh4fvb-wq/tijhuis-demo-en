Contract and tendering

To carry out dredging or bank works, it is essential to gather all relevant information for the job and draw up specifications. This includes the permits required, communication with stakeholders and the presence of protected plant and animal species.

The specifications serve as a contract document between client and contractor. This can follow the RAW system, but also take an integrated form (for example D&C or UAV-GC). We have extensive experience with various forms of contract and types of project.

Award criteria

Together with the client, we can determine the award criteria for a given assignment. Where necessary, we also handle the tendering and contracting of the selected contractor. Thanks to our many years of experience, we know many dredging companies and other contractors, and lines of communication are short.

Curious about the possibilities? Contact our advisers directly or fill in the contact form.
