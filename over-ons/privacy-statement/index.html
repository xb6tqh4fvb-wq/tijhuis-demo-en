PRIVACY STATEMENT TIJHUIS INGENIEURS B.V.


1. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE
This is the privacy statement of Tijhuis Ingenieurs B.V. and the organisations directly affiliated with it.

This privacy statement applies to all persons who have contact with Tijhuis Ingenieurs, with the exception of its employees. More specifically, this means that this privacy statement applies to:
• suppliers, visitors, job applicants, clients, contractors and other relations of Tijhuis Ingenieurs;
• visitors to the websites at https://www.tijhuisingenieurs.nl/, or any domain name affiliated with this domain name.

All these persons are referred to collectively below as 'you' and 'your'. The words 'we' and 'us' refer to Tijhuis Ingenieurs. Chapter 1 explains a number of terms. These terms are capitalised in the privacy statement.

We consider it very important that your Personal Data is handled with care. We naturally comply with all legal rules, including the General Data Protection Regulation ('GDPR'), and we protect your Personal Data carefully and adequately.

We ask you to read this privacy statement carefully. To make it easier to read, we have divided the privacy statement into chapters. The table of contents follows below. By clicking on the topic or chapter, you will go directly to the chapter that is relevant to you.

This privacy statement was last amended on 25 May 2018 and applies from that date.


2. DEFINITIONS

Data subject:

the person to whom the Personal Data being processed relates. In other words: the person whose personal information is being processed.

Special Categories of Personal Data:

Special Categories of Personal Data are data concerning a person's religion or beliefs, race, political opinions, health, sex life and trade union membership.

Third Parties:

(legal) persons who are neither a Data Subject nor part of Tijhuis Ingenieurs, such as (legal) persons with whom we share Personal Data on the basis of an agreement. These Third Parties may be processors or joint Controllers.

Personal Data:

all data relating to an identified or identifiable person. In other words: all information that identifies, or can be used to identify, the Data Subject.

Processing:

anything that can be done with Personal Data, such as collecting, recording, storing, modifying, retrieving, using, disseminating and destroying it.

Controller:

the party that processes the data of Data Subjects for a specific purpose. In this Privacy Statement, this is Tijhuis Ingenieurs.


3. THE PERSONAL DATA WE MAY PROCESS ABOUT YOU
Below, we explain which Personal Data Tijhuis Ingenieurs may process about you.

Data you have provided yourself, such as:
• your contact details (name, address, place of residence, email address, telephone number);
• your gender and date of birth;
• data recorded following a telephone conversation with you;
• data from an email from you;
• payment details;
• data from your CV/information about your employment history.

Data we have received via our websites, newsletters and emails:
• information about the device you used to visit our website;
• your browsing behaviour on the website, such as:
o which information/web pages of ours you have viewed;
o the way in which you navigate through the website;
o whether you open a newsletter or email and which parts of it you click on;
o the dates and times of your visits to our website;
• the operating system you use;
• your browser type and IP address;
• your internet service provider;
• the web address of the website from which you were referred;
• your geolocation;
• the data you have downloaded from the website.

Data we have received from other sources, such as:
• data that is available from public sources and that you have evidently made public, such as on social media and business websites;
• data from the Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce and from the Land Registry.

 

4. WHY DO WE PROCESS YOUR PERSONAL DATA?

We use your Personal Data for the following purposes:
• to verify your identity;
• to improve our services (and their security), including by carrying out checks;
• to inform you of news, developments, services and products from Tijhuis Ingenieurs;
• to evaluate, research and develop our services;
• to contact you with newsletters, if you have signed up for them;
• for internal quality purposes;
• to manage, secure, adapt and improve our websites;
• for marketing/commercial purposes;
• for recruitment and selection;
• to comply with a legal obligation incumbent on Tijhuis Ingenieurs;
• to be able to contact you and respond to the questions you have asked;
• to handle your requests for information and to deal with complaints;
• to prevent theft, misuse, vandalism or other criminal behaviour;
• to be able to carry out satisfaction surveys.

 

5. THE GROUNDS ON WHICH WE PROCESS PERSONAL DATA

To be allowed to use Personal Data, we need a legal basis under the GDPR.
We use the following grounds:
• you have given Tijhuis Ingenieurs your explicit consent;
• the use is necessary to perform or enter into an agreement with you;
• the use is necessary to comply with a legal obligation;
• in emergencies: the use is necessary to protect a vital interest of you or of another person;
• we have a legitimate interest in the use, unless your privacy interest or that of a Third Party outweighs it.

Tijhuis Ingenieurs may rely on the following legitimate interests:
• protecting your safety;
• protecting the safety of clients and/or others, and the buildings and property of Tijhuis Ingenieurs;
• for quality and training purposes;
• promoting IT management and improving security;
• analysing and improving our services and the content of our websites;
• delivering, improving, adapting, supporting, researching and analysing our services;
• promoting the quality and safety of our services;
• communicating with you and other contacts of Tijhuis Ingenieurs;
• preventing and combating fraud, unauthorised use, and breaches of our terms and conditions and policies, or other harmful or illegal activities.

 

6. SHARING PERSONAL DATA WITH THIRD PARTIES
We only share your Personal Data with Third Parties where this is necessary, because Third Parties carry out work on behalf of Tijhuis Ingenieurs, and only for the purposes described in this Privacy Statement. If a Third Party is engaged, Tijhuis Ingenieurs will ensure that this Third Party applies the same level of security and confidentiality as that maintained by us.


Tijhuis Ingenieurs may make use of the following categories of contractors:
• website management;
• HR software suppliers.

Tijhuis Ingenieurs remains responsible for the processing of your Personal Data. Tijhuis Ingenieurs enters into agreements with Third Parties to ensure that your Personal Data is properly secured.

 

7. SECURITY
Tijhuis Ingenieurs takes the protection and security of your Personal Data very seriously and continually takes measures to protect your Personal Data as effectively as possible against unlawful use. Among other things, we have taken the following measures:
• only persons who are authorised to do so have access to Personal Data, and the number of people with access to Personal Data is kept to a minimum;
• all employees of Tijhuis Ingenieurs have signed a confidentiality agreement, in which they undertake to observe complete confidentiality regarding all information (including Personal Data);
• electronic transmission of Personal Data always takes place via a secure connection;
• the website is secured via https;
• we ensure that your Personal Data is only shared with Third Parties if we have agreed appropriate security measures with these Third Parties.

In addition, Tijhuis Ingenieurs ensures that all of its user settings, programmes and services are designed from the start of development in such a way that your privacy protection is maximised.

 

8. AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING
Where necessary, Tijhuis Ingenieurs may take decisions based on automated processing on matters that may have (significant) consequences for you. These are decisions taken by computer programmes or systems, without a person (for example an employee of Tijhuis Ingenieurs) being involved. Example: if fraudulent activity is suspected, we may block you from using our websites.

 

9. YOUR RIGHTS
If your Personal Data is processed, you have a number of rights, which are set out below. You can exercise these rights by submitting your request in writing, addressed to Tijhuis Ingenieurs, or by sending an email to: Myrte Tijhuis info@tijhuisingenieurs.nl. Tijhuis Ingenieurs will verify your identity before your request can be fulfilled.

Tijhuis Ingenieurs will respond substantively to your request as soon as possible, and at the latest within one month. Tijhuis Ingenieurs does not charge you any costs for handling the above-mentioned requests, unless your requests are excessive.

1. Access to Personal Data
You can ask us at any time to state which (categories of) Personal Data Tijhuis Ingenieurs processes about you, for what purposes this is done, from which source the Personal Data originates and which retention periods are applied.

2. Rectification of Personal Data
You can contact us at any time to supplement or correct your Personal Data. Should it unexpectedly turn out that Tijhuis Ingenieurs has processed incorrect Personal Data about you and/or provided it to Third Parties, Tijhuis Ingenieurs will correct this immediately. If Tijhuis Ingenieurs has amended your Personal Data, Tijhuis Ingenieurs will inform you of this.

3. Restricting the processing of your Personal Data
If you disagree with the content of the Personal Data that Tijhuis Ingenieurs holds about you, you can in some cases submit a request to temporarily restrict the processing of your Personal Data.

4. Withdrawing consent
You can withdraw the consent you have given for the processing of your Personal Data. Withdrawing your consent does not affect processing that has already taken place.

5. Right to transfer Personal Data (right to data portability)
You can request the Personal Data that Tijhuis Ingenieurs holds about you in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format. Once you have received the Personal Data, you are free to transfer this information.

6. Right to be forgotten
If you no longer wish to use the services of Tijhuis Ingenieurs, you can submit a request for your Personal Data to be deleted.

7. Right to object
You have the right to object to the processing of your Personal Data by Tijhuis Ingenieurs where this is based on the grounds of 'legitimate interest of Tijhuis Ingenieurs or of a third party' and 'performance of a task carried out in the public interest'. Where Tijhuis Ingenieurs relies on these grounds, Tijhuis Ingenieurs carries out a balancing of interests with regard to your privacy. The right to object gives you the opportunity to require Tijhuis Ingenieurs to carry out this balancing of interests again.

8. Right to lodge a complaint/start legal proceedings
We are committed to working with you to find a fair solution if you have a complaint about or a problem with our use of your Personal Data. You also have the option of lodging a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (the Dutch Data Protection Authority) or starting legal proceedings. The contact details of the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens can be found by clicking here.

10. MINORS ON THE TIJHUIS INGENIEURS WEBSITE
Although visitors of all ages may visit our websites, we do not wish to collect Personal Data from children under the age of 16 without the consent of their parent/guardian. If you are 16 or younger, you may therefore only use our website under the supervision of your parent/guardian.

 

11. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES
Tijhuis Ingenieurs uses cookies and similar technologies on its website. Cookies are small pieces of information that are stored on your computer. We use cookies to make our services function and to provide them. This enables Tijhuis Ingenieurs to see how its visitors use the website. Tijhuis Ingenieurs can therefore adapt its website to user preferences. On your next visit to the Tijhuis Ingenieurs website, these small pieces of information will be recognised.

More information about the cookie policy can be found on our website; cookie statement.

13. CHANGES AND APPLICABLE LAW
Our Privacy Statement may need to be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available on our website. We will announce any changes to the Privacy Statement by email and/or by a clearly visible notice on our website.

None of the provisions of this Privacy Statement is intended to create an obligation or agreement between Tijhuis Ingenieurs and you. The provisions of this Privacy Statement and any disputes arising from it are governed by Dutch law.

 

14. RETENTION PERIODS
Tijhuis Ingenieurs never retains Personal Data for longer than is necessary for the purpose of the processing. Tijhuis Ingenieurs retains your Personal Data for as long as we consider necessary to provide services, enable you to use the website, comply with applicable legislation, resolve disputes with parties and otherwise to the extent necessary to enable us to carry out our business activities.

Tijhuis Ingenieurs will in any case retain your Personal Data for the period during which you have an agreement with Tijhuis Ingenieurs. Statutory retention periods apply to some Personal Data. Tijhuis Ingenieurs fully observes these statutory retention periods.
