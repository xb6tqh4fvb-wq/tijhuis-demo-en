We believe in a Netherlands that is ready for the challenges of climate change through effective water management, so that we create a lasting positive impact. Our passion drives us as the expert in sustainable regional water management, with our core business of 'water beds and dredging' as a cornerstone.

How do we approach our mission?

1. As pioneers in regional water management in the Netherlands, we provide sustainable advice by thinking ahead, connecting and delivering.

With our close-knit team, rich in knowledge and experience from fieldwork to advice and project supervision, we never shy away from a project. We investigate from the core, think ahead, connect disciplines and ensure real-world delivery.

2. We have the knowledge, data and tools in-house for an integrated approach.

Our field of operation covers four key areas of work: water beds and dredging, banks and civil engineering structures, water quality and biodiversity, and W.I.T. and data processing. By working from these areas (with a team of professionals for each one), we take on projects as a whole. With our own software, W.I.T., we bring together knowledge and data, which strengthens sustainability through quality and knowledge assurance.

3. Sustainability runs through the veins of all our employees and is embedded in all our projects.

With their insights, our employees contribute to a more sustainable future. It is in their DNA to take environmental friendliness into account in every phase of a project, so that we continue to safeguard sustainable results.

4. Developing innovative ideas and bringing them to life.

At Tijhuis Ingenieurs, we foster a culture in which innovation is not only encouraged but also actually put into practice. Through our creativity and determination, we develop and implement concrete, innovative solutions that contribute directly to sustainable, future-proof water management in the Netherlands.

 

Specialism

Our specialism is preparing and supervising dredging work. Our fieldworkers are out and about every day. They take samples of the water bed, survey the banks and measure the sediment thickness. With this information, we improve flow and water quality and contribute to greater biodiversity.

We check and apply for permits and communicate with stakeholders. All the information is taken into account when drawing up a specification. We support the contracting of a contractor, and the work is then carried out under the watchful eye of our project supervisor and inspector. After the project is completed, we check the work and provide advice on monitoring and maintenance.

All data from the field is sent straight to the office and stored centrally. The entire process is supported by our own software, W.I.T. This information gives us insight into the maintenance condition and behaviour of the water system. We translate this into the maintenance plan and tailored advice.

Our services and fields of work

Tijhuis Ingenieurs deals with every aspect of water management consultancy.

We provide advice throughout the entire process, from investigation to implementation, and have field teams that carry out measurements and sampling.

See services and fields of expertise for more information.

More than dredging preparation and supervision

But our work doesn't stop there. Remediation, bank design, ecology, water quality and civil engineering structures also fall within our fields of expertise. And because a watercourse often has more than one function, we try to take this into account in our work.

For example, we combine nature restoration with improving water quality. Or we use the preparation for dredging work to gain insight into the maintenance condition of bank revetments.
