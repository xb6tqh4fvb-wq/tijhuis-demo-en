Sustainable advice in and around the water

We believe in a Netherlands that is ready for the challenges of climate change through effective water management, so that we create lasting positive impact. Our passion drives us as the expert in sustainable regional water management, with our core business of 'water beds and dredging' as a pillar.

We are Tijhuis Ingenieurs

An independent and meaningful engineering consultancy in the field of regional water management in the Netherlands. We know everything about water beds, dredging, water quality, biodiversity, banks, civil engineering structures and ecology.

We actively contribute ideas on the best approach to the entire project. Our broad knowledge of both the preparatory research phase and the practical execution phase of dredging works makes us a valued partner for water boards, municipalities and other organisations throughout the Netherlands.

Our field workers can be found throughout the Netherlands for measurements and surveys. Think of sounding the sludge thickness, sampling the water bed, water bed investigations and surveys of flora and fauna.

Our consultants provide advice throughout the entire process, from investigation to execution. They design nature-friendly banks, advise on the processing of dredged sediment, apply for permits and draw up specifications .

Our supervisors and project managers ensure that quality is safeguarded during execution. They are the link between client and contractor.

Tijhuis Ingenieurs is certified to carry out fieldwork for environmental water bed investigations (BRL 2003) and for the environmental supervision of water bed remediation (BRL 6003).
