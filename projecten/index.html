Zeven Stromen

In the Zeven Stromen series, we take you through seven projects where technology, ecology and environment come together. From research and preparation to smart choices that make the difference out in the field.

Working together for clean water in ’t Hol

In the ’t Hol nature reserve, we are removing 200,000 m³ of dredged sediment from the ditches to make the water cleaner and give space to plants such as water soldier and water lilies. Tijhuis Ingenieurs supervises the nature-friendly execution from the water. Through better flow and a thin layer of iron sludge, we are improving water quality and limiting algal growth. The project runs until the summer of 2027, after which nature can recover.

Supervision of the Lunenburgerwaard nature development

Lunenburgerwaard nature development

The Lunenburgerwaard nature development allows the area to develop further. In this way, the nature reserve meets the requirements of a Natura 2000 site and the Nature Network Netherlands. Biodiversity will increase and nature will become stronger.

Remediation of Ringvaart Oost

After the contaminated dredged sediment was removed, the water bed of the Ringvaart Oost in Amsterdam was capped with bentonite mats. The film accompanying this article shows how the mat is hoisted into the water and how divers guide the mat to the bottom.

Increasing water supply and constructing nature-friendly banks

Waterschap Rivierenland has constructed three kilometres of nature-friendly bank and installed a new, larger culvert to improve water management in the area behind it. We took care of the preparation, the design and the contract phase.

Area-wide water quality study for Noorderzijlvest

Waterschap Noorderzijlvest wants to contribute to improving water quality through its regular dredging work. To this end, Tijhuis Ingenieurs has carried out an area-wide study of the nutrients in the water bed and their release into the surface water.

Survey of the Overijssels Kanaal

Waterschap Drents Overijsselse Delta wants to redevelop the Overijssels Kanaal between Deventer and Raalte over a length of more than 20 kilometres. With the dredging and deepening of the canal, the creation of five kilometres of nature-friendly banks and the repair and replacement of the bank structures, it is a major undertaking.

This article discusses the project approach and also describes new developments.

Construction management and supervision at Lorentzhaven, Harderwijk

The Lorentzhaven in Harderwijk has been dredged and fitted with new sheet pile walings. Tijhuis Ingenieurs provided the construction management and supervised the work.

Restoration and sustainable preservation of the Kievitsbuurten ridge-and-furrow landscape

Tijhuis Ingenieurs is currently working on a special project: a pilot for the restoration and sustainable preservation of the so-called 'legakker' (ridge-and-furrow) landscape of the Kievitsbuurten near Breukelen. To preserve this unique cultural-historical landscape, the municipality of Stichtse Vecht, in cooperation with the Plassenschap Loosdrecht, Waterschap AGV and the Kievitsbuurten association, wants to carry out pilots to trial various bank protection techniques in combination with reed restoration.

Redevelopment of Eilandenboulevard Amsterdam: a project with a variety of challenges

A unique project in which various disciplines of Tijhuis Ingenieurs were brought together: surveying the slope and water bed, designing the bank facilities and jetties, communicating with residents, arranging permits and providing technical support during construction.

The specialist in sustainable regional water management

Tijhuis Ingenieurs is the specialist in regional water management. We have been experts in preparing and supervising dredging works for more than 20 years. In this video you can see how we go about it.

Room for submerged aquatic plants

To give more space for the development of submerged aquatic plants, Waterschap Rivierenland plans to widen and deepen a number of watercourses in its management area. Tijhuis Ingenieurs is carrying out the preparatory studies.

Bank reconstruction in Nieuwegein

Preparation of specifications, project management and supervision for the replacement of sheet piling along the Gekanaliseerde Hollandse IJssel.

Study in the Eemsgeul

The Eemsgeul in the Wadden Sea is dredged continuously to ensure sufficient depth for ships. Water bed investigations therefore need to be carried out.

Tijhuis products

Supervision of water bed remediation in Surhuisterveen

Water bed remediation has been carried out in Surhuisterveen. We supervised the work.

Dredging work in West Maas en Waal

The municipality of West Maas en Waal received an inspection letter from the water board, which showed that there was a backlog of maintenance. Tijhuis Ingenieurs prepared all the dredging work for the client.

New WIT users

In 2018 we once again provided 4 new users with their own digital environment: the municipalities of Woerden, Alphen a/d Rijn and Houten, and Hoogheemraadschap de Stichtse Rijnlanden.

Carrying out fieldwork with WIT Metingen
