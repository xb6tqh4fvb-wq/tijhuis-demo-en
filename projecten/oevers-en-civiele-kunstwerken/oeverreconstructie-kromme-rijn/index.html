Bank reconstruction Kromme Rijn

Over a length of approximately 300 metres, the bank protection along the Kromme Rijn in Wijk bij Duurstede is no longer functional. On behalf of Hoogheemraadschap de Stichtse Rijnlanden, Tijhuis Ingenieurs therefore prepared, tendered and supervised the replacement of the sheet piling. A sustainable structure was designed, the works were coordinated with private owners, and the project was supported by our contract manager and site supervisor.

Inventory of the existing situation

The bank protection to be replaced lies on the edge of the urban area of Wijk bij Duurstede. Along this stretch of the Kromme Rijn, many private gardens extend right down to the water. During preparation, we therefore mapped out carefully which owners needed to be informed ahead of the works.

For the technical design, cross-sections were measured, cone penetration tests and boreholes were carried out, and the location of objects was recorded. In addition, soil and water bed quality were determined and a flora and fauna survey was carried out.

Design of the new bank protection

A sustainable solution was sought for the new structure. For this purpose, an anchored sheet pile wall was designed, consisting of recycled plastic with softwood piles. The visible top section is finished with a timber waling and capping plank.

Tendering and supervision of the works

The work was put out to a multiple private tender and awarded on the basis of BPKV (Best Price-Quality Ratio). A sustainable working method (CO2 Ladder, processing of released materials) and good stakeholder management were assessed positively. During supervision of the works, the challenges were:
• The temporary strong current in the Kromme Rijn for water supply to the hinterland,
• Dealing with rubble in the bank and subsoil,
• Communication with residents and agreements on the handover,
• Removal of the asbestos-containing bank protection.

For the replacement of the sheet piling, terraces, jetties, fences and vegetation had to be removed temporarily. Coordination with the owners and good communication during the works were very important. After completion, all private facilities were formalised in permits.
