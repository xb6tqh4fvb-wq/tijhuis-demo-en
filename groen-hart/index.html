One ambition: to be the leading expert in sustainable regional water management by 2030

Regional water management is about far more than having enough water in the right place. Water quality, water beds, nature, material use and climate all come together.

That is why, in our projects, we look beyond the immediate task. Where can we improve water quality? What opportunities are there for nature? Can released materials be reused? And how can work be carried out with less impact?

Met kennis, data en ervaring uit de praktijk vertalen we die vragen naar onderbouwde en uitvoerbare oplossingen. Zo werken we stap voor stap aan onze ambitie.

Five themes for working step by step towards restorative value for people, water and landscape.

water Water (bed) local_florist Nature luggage Materials eco Climate impact bolt Empowered by your strengths
Water
Water and water bed quality are at the heart of our work. We investigate what is happening within a water system and identify opportunities and bottlenecks. We translate that knowledge into measures for the sustainable management of water and water beds.
Nature
A healthy water system and healthy nature go hand in hand. That is why, in our projects, we look at ways to preserve and strengthen biodiversity. For example, with nature-friendly banks and measures that improve habitats for plants and animals.
Materials
Materials that are released can often be reused. That is why we look for high-quality applications for dredged sediment and other material flows. In this way we retain value and, wherever possible, give materials a new purpose.
Climate impact
We want to reduce the climate impact of our work. That means making conscious choices in projects and within our own organisation, for example in relation to energy use, transport, equipment and the way we organise our work.
Empowered by your strengths
For us, sustainability is also about the people who do the work. We invest in development, collaboration, job satisfaction and a safe working environment in which colleagues can use their strengths and continue to develop.
We believe in meaningful work: building together towards a future in which nature and water reinforce each other.

Our sustainability ambition does not stand still. We continually review where we stand, what can be improved and which steps are needed next. In this video, Myrte explains how we are working on this at Tijhuis and how sustainability is becoming ever more embedded in our projects and organisation.
